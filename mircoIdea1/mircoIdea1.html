Micro Idea 1

I want this interaction to feel calming and tranquil. I want the input to be a button that you can press and a flower or tree will appear. The change over time would be if you click the button the flower would appear and then maybe slowly disappear or you don't click the button and then nothing will appear. The unevenness would appear if you do not click the button the flower will not appear at all. I want the overal sketch to feel peaceful and so my mind goes to nature.
