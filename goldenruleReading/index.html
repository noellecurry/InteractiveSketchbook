Shneiderman's 8 Golden Rules

Absent Rule

The rule that feels most absent from my micro-project page is rule number 6: Permit easy reversal of actions. This also goes in line with Nielsen’s Heuristics when he talks about user control and freedom, and my micro project does not have an easy way for users to stop interaction.

Most Improve Sketchbook Interface

The rule that I believe would enrich my sketchbook the most is rule number 1: Strive for Consistency. I believe this because when navigating throughout my sketchbook some pages have titles, and some do not. As well as the font size differs throughout my pages, I think that having a consistent font hierarchy and structure of my pages would really help a user navigate through my sketchbook.

Hardest Rule

I believe that the hardest rule for me to implement is rule number 5: Prevent Errors. I also felt this way when doing the Nielsen's Usability Heuristics reading when he talked about error preventions. I think I have a much better understanding of what it is, but I don’t know where I would implement that because at least for my sketchbook it is a very passive experience.

Contradictions?

I do not believe any of these rules contradict how I designed my interactive sketch book or micro project. I found this reading to be very similar to the 10 Nielsen's Usability Heuristics and the chapter 1 reading we did previously.
