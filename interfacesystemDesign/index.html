Project 2 - Interface System Design

Figma Board

This interface was designed to be as simple and user-friendly as possible while still maintaining a design that looks good. For the navigation, I have a simple hamburger menu that will pull out from left to right and have all the pages organized by week. When users click one of the weeks, they will be taken to a screen that is one long scroll that has all the sketches a part of that week. I did this with heuristic number 3: user control and freedom in mind because this will help users not feel so overwhelmed by seeing all the pages at once and instead get to scroll and explore the different weeks.

Another design I implemented is having the title of the site “interactive sketchbook” vertical on the left side of the screen that is there on every page and the navigation for desktop view. This feature is consistent across every page, including within the navigation itself, making it a stable and recognizable anchor within the design. I believe that because it is on every screen the user gets taken to including the navigation, it is a signifier for the user that an affordance is there. I also believe that this could fall into heuristic number 7: flexibility and efficiency of use because this may be a shortcut hidden to inexperienced users.

I believe my hierarchy logic is very clear to users as I have the most important information large and at the top and the structure of the pages are consistent. The most important information is presented prominently at the top of each page, with size, placement, and spacing used intentionally to guide the user’s attention. This consistent structure helps users quickly understand where to look and how to engage with the content, reinforcing heuristic number 4: consistency and standards and heuristic number 6: recognition rather than recall. Because of this consistency, users do not have to relearn how to navigate or interpret each page, making the experience feel more seamless and predictable. This reduces cognitive load and allows users to focus more on the content itself rather than the interface.

The mobile view was slightly challenging for me because I had to take an already stripped-down design and take it even further. I approached this by keeping the layout largely the same but removing the vertical “interactive sketchbook” title from most pages to preserve space and clarity on a smaller screen. I only included it on the homepage and within the navigation, while keeping its placement consistent so it still feels connected to the overall design system. This decision helps maintain usability while adapting to the constraints of mobile devices. Altogether, these design decisions aim to create an experience that is intuitive, flexible, and visually balanced, allowing users to confidently explore the interactive sketchbook in a way that feels natural but also evokes curiosity.
