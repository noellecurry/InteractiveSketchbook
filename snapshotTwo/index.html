Some structural designs I have made is really honing in what I want my navigation bar to look like especially for the mobile device. I want my sketch to be as minimal and simple as possible and really thinking about the readings that we did. During my sketchbook audit I said I felt that my interface violated is heuristic number three user control and freedom and heuristic number five error preventions. So, with this in mind I am thinking about all the affordances apart of my sketchbook and the signifiers I have to show to users that they are there. With my navigation, I have all the different pages in subcategories separated by week so that when users click, another drop down menu will appear with all the individual pages. I also have my interactive sketchbook titled vertical on the screen for my homage and on my navigation so that it is always there on that part of the webpage, but when users start going through all the pages, the vertical title will only be on the navigation. Not too much has changed from my previous sketch because I was really focused on the navigation bar, and I think that I still want to go with this very simple minimal design with no excess. What still feels unresolved is heuristic number 5 error prevention and supporting the undo. Specifically I have in mind the users navigating throughout my pages and giving them the confidence to explore my pages. I need to brainstorm more how I can do this for users. I was also thinking about this because of my “interactive sketchbook” that sits vertically; I want to be how users can get back home. But having a signifier to users so they know that affordance is there I think is important so understanding how I can implement this is my next step.
